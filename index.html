Monograph Arc: The Inner Logic

The monograph follows a four-level ontological progression implicit in the Rig Veda and rediscovered in modern mathematics[cite: 1]:

  • Process → Agni → unbounded operators[cite: 1]
  • Law → Ṛta → naturality, universality[cite: 1]
  • Manifestation → Nāma–Rūpa → spectral theory[cite: 1]
  • Invariance → Akṣara (future volume) → limits, fixed points[cite: 1]

The present monograph completes levels 1–3 rigorously, while opening level 4[cite: 1].

Core Achievements

  • Establishes a non-mythological, rigorous Rig-Vedic ontology[cite: 1].
  • Reinterprets functional analysis at the foundational level[cite: 1].
  • Reframes law as coherence, not causation[cite: 1].
  • Provides a conceptual bridge usable by physicists, mathematicians, and philosophers[cite: 1].
